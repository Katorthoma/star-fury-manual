# Getting started

Star Fury rewards players who understand their constraints before they spend. Your first session is not about finding a perfect build order. It is about learning what your empire can produce, what it can build, and how long every decision takes.

## What kind of game is Star Fury?

You control an empire rather than an individual ship. Over a round, you will:

- develop an economy
- expand across Land and Asteroids
- research technologies
- build and configure ships
- explore
- gather intelligence with Probes
- raid computer-controlled targets
- fight alliance Boss Battles
- attack and defend against other empires
- contribute to alliance upgrades and recovery
- potentially die and respawn

The strategic core is the interaction of **time, production, information, and force**.

A fleet built too early can choke the economy with maintenance. A fleet built too late may finish after the battle that mattered. Research completed early can compound for many ticks. Exploration creates future production but can leave a ship unavailable when war begins.

Before most actions, ask:

> What does this cost now, what does it produce later, and when do I need the resource or ship again?

## Think in ticks

Horizon LIII uses **1-hour ticks**. Construction, research, ship return time, exploration, and many cooldowns are measured in ticks. Other rounds can use a different tick speed.

A 20-tick technology is not simply "20 hours away." It also occupies the research slot while another technology waits.

A 36-tick ship started shortly before war may contribute nothing to the opening fight.

A production upgrade completed early can pay you every remaining tick of the round.

!!! tip "Planning note"
    When comparing two options, check five things: time, immediate cost, per-tick return, the alternative you delay, and the next deadline.

## Your first 15 minutes

Use this sequence to build a map of your empire before you optimize it.

### 1. Read the top bar

Start with the visible summary:

- Credits
- Power
- Population
- Asteroids
- Land
- Networth and rank
- News

These values tell you where you are, not why. The Production page explains the flow behind them.

### 2. Open production

Production is the fastest way to understand the economy. Check:

- Power Production
- Population Consumed Power
- Net Power Production
- Metal Production
- Deuterium Production
- Iridium Production
- Population Per Tick
- Population Capacity
- Recruits Per Tick
- Probe Production
- Credit Production
- Military Ship Running Costs
- Credits Per Tick

Pay special attention to the difference between **Credit Production** and **Credits Per Tick**. Ship maintenance comes out every tick, so a fleet can be affordable to build and expensive to keep.

### 3. Open research

Check:

- Research Points per tick
- active research
- Research Queue, if your VIP tier provides one
- completed technologies
- available technologies
- category limits

Races can start with different research. Do not assume another empire has the same opening tree.

### 4. Inspect buildings

Check how much Land and Asteroid capacity is free and which resource each existing building produces.

Building prices are dynamic. Use the price shown on your Buildings page rather than copying a value from an old guide.

### 5. Inspect the Star Dock

Check:

- Cadets available
- Recruits available to train
- Attack Dock capacity
- Defence Dock capacity
- Raider Dock capacity
- Leecher Dock capacity
- Build Dock capacity
- ships that are Building, Returning, or Disabled
- each ship's W/E/S allocation

A ship can exist and still contribute nothing useful if it is in the wrong state or configured for the wrong job.

### 6. Check protection and politics

Open Preferences and Universe News.

A new empire receives **48 hours of Newbie protection from Tick 1**. A respawned empire receives another 48 hours.

Do not leave Newbie protection because the button is available. Leave it when a specific action requires the risk.

### 7. Connect with the community

Open **Comms → Discord** in-game to reach the Star Fury community.

Discord is where many players find alliances, organize raid groups, coordinate wars and attacks, share intelligence, ask questions, and help one another. The in-game Discord link is preferable to a copied invite because it remains under the game's control.

### 8. Claim the daily reward

The daily reward is **75 Reward Points**. Reward Points survive death and respawn.

Redeem them against your bottleneck instead of choosing the resource with the largest-looking number.

## Your first decision

After the walkthrough, identify one constraint:

- Credits
- Power
- population capacity
- Research Points
- Metal
- Deuterium
- territory
- information

Solve that constraint before trying to maximize everything at once.
