# Newbie protection and war timing

## Newbie protection

A new empire receives **48 hours of Newbie protection from Tick 1**. A respawned empire receives another **48 hours**.

Newbie status appears in the Universe Browser and can be left voluntarily through Preferences.

Protection is an asset. Before leaving it, ask:

- What action requires me to leave?
- Are the ships I need home?
- Is the alliance ready?
- Do I understand the war state?
- Can my economy replace losses?
- Have likely enemies scanned or targeted me?

There is no reward for becoming vulnerable early.

## War preparation checklist

Before a scheduled war or peace expiration:

1. Bring important ships home.
2. Leave a buffer for return time.
3. Finish necessary role transfers and Fixed/Flexible conversions.
4. Return explorers and rearm S100 ships to a combat Weapons allocation.
5. Check Current Defence, not only Max Possible Defence.
6. Refresh intelligence on likely targets.
7. Calculate Break Orders.
8. Calculate attacker survival against retaliation.
9. Train affordable Cadets; trained Cadets survive death and respawn.
10. Reserve Power and shipbuilding resources for replacements.
11. Avoid long, non-cancellable exploration.
12. Confirm alliance target order and war assignments.
13. Check shields and any other War Technology the alliance enabled.

The battle report is an expensive place to discover that a key ship was still Returning.
