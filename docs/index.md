# Star Fury V5 Player Manual

**An unofficial community manual for new and returning Star Fury players.**

Star Fury is a persistent, tick-based strategy game. You build an economy, expand territory, research technology, configure fleets, gather intelligence, raid, and coordinate alliance warfare.

This manual documents V5. When a value can change by round or administrator action, the page marks it as round-specific.

## Community and support

Star Fury is a social game. Much of a round's coordination happens between players as well as through the game interface.

**Discord**  
Open **Comms → Discord** in-game to join the Star Fury community. Players use Discord to find alliances, coordinate raids and wars, plan attacks, share intelligence, ask questions, and help newer players.

**Support Star Fury**  
You can support Star Fury directly through the developer's [Patreon](https://www.patreon.com/starfury).

This manual is an unofficial community project and is not affiliated with or funded by Star Fury or its Patreon.

!!! info "Evidence-driven documentation"
    Confirmed mechanics are stated directly. Round-specific, calculated, historical, and unverified material is labeled where it matters. See [Evidence and accuracy](about/evidence.md).

## Operating principles

- **Think in ticks.** Time is a resource.
- **Grow what compounds.** Early production pays for the rest of the round.
- **Build ships for jobs.** A fleet without a purpose is an expensive Networth display.
- **Scan before you commit force.** Networth is not a combat report.

And when the game offers an irreversible button, assume it has been waiting 25 years for you to click it without reading.
