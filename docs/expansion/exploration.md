# Exploration and expansion

Exploration converts an eligible Defence Dock ship and Exploration Points into additional **Land or Asteroids**.

## Requirements

A ship must:

- be in the **Defence Dock**
- be configured to **S100**

Engine allocation does not change exploration duration.

## Mission lengths

| Mission | Duration |
|---|---:|
| Short | 4 ticks |
| Medium | 6 ticks |
| Long | 8 ticks |

**Exploration missions cannot be cancelled.**

!!! warning "Before you send a ship"
    Check the next war, raid, or role-change deadline first. An 8-tick mission commits the ship for the full 8 ticks, and you may still need another 2 ticks afterward to reconfigure it from S100 to a combat Weapons allocation.

## Exploration Points

The Exploration page shows your Exploration Points, eligible ships, estimated Short/Medium/Long yields, and the choice between Land and Asteroids.

!!! question "Unverified"
    The formula that generates Exploration Points and converts EP into the displayed territory estimates is not yet fully decoded.

Use the live estimate for the mission you are about to send.

## Choose the mission by return, not size

A larger mission is not automatically a better mission.

For a simple efficiency check:

`expected territory / mission ticks`

Then value the territory itself. If your Asteroid economy is starving for Metal, an Asteroid mission may be worth more than a slightly larger Land result. If Research Labs, Residents, or Fusion Plants are the constraint, Land may be more valuable.
