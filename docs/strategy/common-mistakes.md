# Common mistakes

Most expensive mistakes in Star Fury are not mysterious. They come from committing time or resources before checking what the action actually changes.

## Building too much military too early

Ships cost resources twice: once to build and every tick to maintain.

A fleet that produces no immediate strategic value can delay the economy and research that would have made the later fleet stronger.

## Treating Networth as combat strength

Networth does not tell you ship status, Hull, Shields, W/E/S, dock role, Probes, research, Current Defence, or whether the fleet is home.

**Scan the target.**

## Confusing Attack and Damage

Attack passes the Defence threshold. Damage kills ships.

You can have enough of one and still fail because you lack the other.

## Treating Defence as armor

Defence is not subtracted from incoming Damage. In V5 it acts as a threshold and as retaliation.

## Ignoring ship count

Damage is distributed across active defenders, and retaliation is divided across attackers.

That makes ship count tactical. Cheap attackers can dilute retaliation; weak defenders can absorb overkill that never spills to the next ship.

## Building every ship Flexible

Flexible is not "Fixed, but better." It costs exactly **5% Attack, Damage, and Defence** for role mobility.

Use Flexible when you expect the ship to change jobs. Otherwise, Fixed preserves combat performance.

## Building Flexible Leechers

Leecher cannot change role, so the primary benefit of Flexible is absent.

## Forgetting W/E/S

At W0, Attack and Damage are zero, which also wipes normal combat Defence contribution.

A W0 ship can be physically present and strategically irrelevant.

## Sending long exploration before war

Exploration cannot be cancelled.

Star Fury is unsympathetic to discovering this three ticks after you change your mind.

## Failing to train Cadets before war

Trained Cadets survive death and carry into the respawn.

If you can afford the training without blocking a more urgent pre-war need, converting available Recruits before a dangerous war is usually valuable. A respawned empire has less Population and a weaker economy; preserved Cadets let it crew replacement ships immediately.

## Using stale intelligence

A scan is a timestamped observation, not eternal truth. Ships move, repair, die, return, and change configuration.

## Taking the strongest raid mercenary automatically

Mercenaries consume payout. The best mercenary is normally the weakest one that clears the threshold with a sensible margin.

## Donating to the Station without understanding alliance needs

The Alliance Station is both a shared upgrade system and a recovery system.

Donated resources can become alliance-wide Attack/Defence bonuses, larger or faster Station shipyards, ship technology, and replacement ships for recovering members.

A Station-built ship can be sent to an empire at or below alliance average Networth.

!!! tip "Planning note"
    Ask what the alliance needs before donating. Cadets are often valuable because Station ships still require crews. Treat donations as alliance logistics, not automatic dumping and not something to avoid by default.
