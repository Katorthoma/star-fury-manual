# Star Fury V5 manual visual plan

The manual should look like technical documentation first and a game site second. Visuals should teach location, state, or workflow. They should not decorate pages that are already clear in prose.

## Visual rules

- Use a screenshot when it answers **where is this**, **what am I looking at**, or **what changes when I click this**.
- Crop to the relevant interface region. Avoid full-page captures unless layout context is the lesson.
- Prefer numbered annotations over paragraphs of callout text baked into an image.
- Keep annotations stable. Put volatile numbers in the caption or prose, not inside permanent graphics.
- Add useful alt text that describes the interface content and purpose.
- Avoid showing private alliance chat, unnecessary player names, or unrelated empire data.
- Use screenshots at native or high-enough resolution that tables remain readable when opened.
- Do not put two screenshots where one annotated screenshot will do.

## Recommended figure set

### 1. Production: reading the economy

**Page:** Getting started or Resources and economy  
**Crop:** Production summary  
**Annotate:** gross Credits, military running cost, net Credits/tick, Power production/consumption, Recruit production  
**Purpose:** teach the difference between stockpiles and per-tick flow.

### 2. Buildings: capacity and construction state

**Page:** Buildings and production  
**Crop:** one Asteroid section and one Land section  
**Annotate:** Built, Building, live cost, available territory  
**Purpose:** show that in-progress buildings are future output and construction cost is dynamic.

### 3. Star Dock: the five jobs

**Page:** Ships  
**Crop:** Star Dock role overview  
**Annotate:** Attack, Defence, Raider, Leecher, Build  
**Purpose:** make role separation visual before the reader learns role math.

### 4. Ship Viewer: W/E/S and Fixed/Flexible

**Page:** Ships  
**Crop:** one representative ship detail panel  
**Annotate:** role, Fixed/Flexible, W/E/S, return time, status  
**Purpose:** connect configuration to live stats and available actions.

### 5. Exploration

**Page:** Exploration and expansion  
**Crop:** mission selector  
**Annotate:** S100 requirement, 4/6/8-tick choices, Land/Asteroid choice, estimated yield  
**Purpose:** show the commitment before the reader clicks a non-cancellable mission.

### 6. Defence

**Page:** Defence  
**Crop:** top Defence summary  
**Annotate:** Current Defence, Max Possible Defence, shield controls, Defence Platforms  
**Purpose:** prevent readers from confusing potential with live protection.

### 7. Raid setup

**Page:** Raids  
**Crop:** 8-player Raid Overview  
**Annotate:** Fleet One, Fleet Two, roles, payout, readiness, Attack/Defence totals  
**Purpose:** make the two-fleet structure and payout system understandable at a glance.

### 8. Raid mercenaries

**Page:** Raids  
**Crop:** Add Raid Mercenary  
**Annotate:** Low/Medium/High strength and 10/13/15% payout  
**Purpose:** show the trade between threshold safety and human payout.

### 9. Intelligence Center

**Page:** Intelligence and universe  
**Crop:** scan selector and Probe/Command Probe counters  
**Annotate:** scan type, Probe pool, Command Probes, target  
**Purpose:** teach what limits scanning cadence.

### 10. Boss Battle wave

**Page:** Boss Battles  
**Crop:** an active Boss wave  
**Annotate:** Boss, wave type, requirement, contribution control  
**Purpose:** show that Boss Battles are sequential alliance tasks, not a single fleet check.

### 11. Alliance Station combat upgrades

**Page:** Alliance Station  
**Status:** included  
**Purpose:** show how donations become shared Attack/Defence and Star Dock capability.

### 12. Alliance Station shipyard upgrades

**Page:** Alliance Station  
**Status:** included  
**Purpose:** show capacity, Big Hammer, and ship-technology investment.

### 13. Attack and Break Calculator

**Page:** PvP combat  
**Crop:** attack selection and/or Break Calculator  
**Annotate:** target Defence, attacker count, Break Order output  
**Purpose:** bridge the formulas to the interface players actually use.

## Annotation style

Use small numbered circles and a legend below the image. Keep color use restrained: the site accent color for neutral markers, warning color only for actual danger/irreversibility.

Captions should explain the lesson rather than repeat the alt text.
