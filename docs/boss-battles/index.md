# Boss Battles

Boss Battles are a separate **War Room** activity from Raids. Both are available during peacetime, but they use different mechanics, participation requirements, and progression.

## Boss types

V5 has four named Bosses:

- **Alpha**
- **Delta**
- **Sigma**
- **Omega**

Bosses are completed as an alliance.

---

## Boss wave types

Depending on the Boss, a battle can require several wave types.

### Intel Attack

Requires probes and General Scan.

### Damage Attack

Requires Attack Dock ships.

### Leech Attack

Requires Leecher Dock ships.

### EMP Attack

Requires Power.

Once a wave is beaten, another wave opens until the Boss is defeated.

---

## Boss progression

Boss Battles are sequential alliance encounters rather than one giant combat check.

An Omega battle in Horizon LIII progressed through multiple wave types and reached a Leecher wave later in the sequence.

An empire can therefore be valuable by contributing the resource required by its assigned wave rather than having the strongest overall fleet.

---

## Horizon LIII Omega reward examples

Boss rewards can differ between empires participating in the same Boss defeat.

Two Horizon LIII Omega payouts were:

| Reward | Omega example A | Omega example B |
|---|---:|---:|
| Credits | 1,200,000 | 1,400,000 |
| Power | 6,000 | 7,000 |
| Metal | 9,000 | 10,500 |
| Deuterium | 6,000 | 7,000 |
| Iridium | 2,400 | 2,800 |
| Cadets | 600 | 700 |
| Land | None | 150 |
| Asteroids | 100 | 100 |

The second package is **16.7% higher** across Credits, Power, Metal, Deuterium, Iridium and Cadets.

The exact logic that assigns an empire to a particular reward step is not yet known.

The evidence does **not** show a simple application of the Alliance Catch Up multiplier to Boss rewards.
