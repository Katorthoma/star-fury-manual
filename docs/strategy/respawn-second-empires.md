# Defeat, respawn, and second empires

## Defeat is not the end

A defeated empire can respawn and receives **48 hours of Newbie protection**.

Respawn research can improve:

- starting territory
- starting resources
- Probe retention
- raid capability

Trained **Cadets survive death and carry into the respawn**. This matters because the returning empire has less Population and a weaker economy than the empire that died.

The alliance can also accelerate recovery. Station-built ships can be sent to an empire while its Networth is **at or below alliance average Networth**.

VIP can change respawn options, including the ability to change race.

## Second empires

V5 supports a second empire.

Confirmed rules:

- both empires must belong to the same alliance
- you cannot operate both simultaneously
- each empire has its own race, research, economy, and fleet
- the second empire cannot simply be deleted independently
- if it is defeated and not respawned, it can disappear

!!! question "Unverified"
    The exact scoring treatment for running two empires has not been verified well enough to publish as a fixed percentage.

A second empire is not free efficiency. It is another strategic position to fund, coordinate, and keep useful.
