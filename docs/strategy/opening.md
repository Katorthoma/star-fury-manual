# New-player opening

There is no universal opening that ignores race, research, and round state. A good opening is a sequence of questions: what constrains you, what compounds, and what must be ready before the next deadline?

## Phase 1: learn your empire

Before committing to a build order:

- read your race modifiers
- inspect starting research
- read Production
- inspect Buildings
- inspect Star Dock capacity
- confirm Newbie protection
- glance at Universe Scores
- read Universe News

At this stage, information is worth more than speed.

## Phase 2: solve the first bottleneck

Early production compounds. Typical constraints are:

- Power
- Credit income
- Population capacity
- Research Points
- Metal
- Deuterium

Do not maximize everything at once. Fix the resource that blocks the next valuable action.

## Phase 3: establish exploration

A cheap ship in the **Defence Dock** at **S100** can turn idle fleet capacity into more territory.

Before you send it, compare:

- mission duration
- estimated yield
- Land vs Asteroid need
- the next war or raid commitment

Exploration cannot be cancelled.

## Phase 4: choose research by timing

Early research usually falls into two useful categories:

**Compounding research** increases production for many future ticks.

**Constraint-solving research** unlocks the ship, Probe system, transfer capability, or other mechanic you need next.

A technology can be powerful and still be the wrong research now.

## Phase 5: join the information economy

Once scanning matters:

- learn what each scan reports
- watch Universe Average Probes
- share useful intelligence with the alliance
- distrust stale scans
- distinguish ship status from theoretical fleet strength

A fresh scan can be worth more than another small ship.

## Phase 6: expand the fleet deliberately

Every ship should have a job. Examples include:

- explorer
- Defender
- Attack ship
- Raider
- Leecher
- flexible reserve
- sacrificial combat hull
- Boss contributor
- Station recovery ship

If the only justification is "it raises Networth," reconsider the build.

## Build repeatable coordination

Once you raid regularly, a stable group can outperform ad-hoc recruiting simply by reducing coordination cost.

The 6-hour cooldown makes a shared schedule useful for groups aiming to complete all 3 daily raids. A group that already knows its Leader, Intel, mercenary, and Leecher assignments spends less time assembling a party and more time finishing raids.
