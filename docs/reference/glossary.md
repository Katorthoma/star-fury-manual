# Glossary

**BO / Break Order**  
A sequence of Damage thresholds needed to destroy successive layers of defenders.

**Cadets**  
Trained Recruits used as ship crew and in Alliance Station construction.

**Command Probe**  
A scan-use resource. Each intelligence scan consumes 1 Command Probe; Command Probes regenerate at 1 per tick up to the race maximum.

**Current Defence**  
The Defence an empire contributes under its present ship states and W/E/S configurations.

**Defence Platform**  
A defensive system on the Defence page. Each completed platform contributes 15 Defence.

**EP / Exploration Points**  
The resource used by Exploration. The generation and yield-scaling formula is not yet fully decoded.

**Fixed**  
A ship state with no Flexible combat penalty. Changing to or from Flexible takes 4 ticks.

**Flexible**  
A ship state that permits compatible role transfers at a -5% Attack, Damage, and Defence penalty.

**HIGH range**  
A harder raid-target range triggered when the Raider Dock crosses a target-specific strength threshold.

**HP**  
Current Hull + current Shields.

**IGT**  
In-game time.

**Intel**  
Information gathered through Probes and scan technologies; also a raid role.

**Leecher**  
A specialized ship and raid role that cannot change role.

**Networth**  
A general empire-value measure. It is not equivalent to live military strength.

**Probes**  
Units used for intelligence and raid Intel checks.

**Raid**  
A PvE operation against a generated War Room target.

**Recruits**  
Population-derived manpower that can be trained into Cadets.

**RP**  
Research Points.

**Station**  
The Alliance Station, shared alliance infrastructure used for upgrades and shipbuilding.

**Support Attack / Support Defence / Support Intel**  
Second-fleet roles in 8-player raids. Support Attack adds Raid Attack, Support Defence adds Raid Defence, and Support Intel adds another Probe contribution plus the same 50%/50% ship contribution as Intel.

**Tick**  
A game update interval used for production, construction, return time, research, and other timers.

**W/E/S**  
Weapons / Engines / Sensors ship Power allocation.
