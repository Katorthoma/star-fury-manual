# Alliances

Alliances are the layer where individual empires become a war economy. They coordinate diplomacy, intelligence, Boss Battles, Station upgrades, target order, and post-war recovery.

## Alliance Center

The Alliance Center includes:

- invitations and requests
- War Room access
- alliance list
- alliance structure
- member list

Alliance roles include:

- Alliance Leader
- Commander
- Station Manager
- Ship Yard Manager
- War Leader
- Break Leader

The game shows the limit for each role.

## Alliance News

Alliance News records alliance-relevant events and retains them for **48 hours**.

Colors are:

- Orange: attacks from your alliance
- Blue: failed enemy attacks
- Yellow: successful enemy attacks
- Red: an empire or Boss was defeated
- Purple: Boss reward notification

## Alliance Forums

Alliance Forums provide persistent coordination inside the game.

They are useful for information that should outlive a chat message, especially scans, target notes, and recurring instructions.

!!! tip "Coordination note"
    Real-time chat is excellent for executing a plan. Persistent forum threads are better for information the next player needs to find six hours later.

## Discord coordination

Many alliances use the Star Fury Discord for real-time coordination. Open **Comms → Discord** in-game to reach the official community entry point.

Discord is especially useful for:

- war planning and target order
- timed attacks and Break Orders
- raid-group coordination
- intelligence sharing
- alliance recruitment and diplomacy
- getting help from other players

Treat Discord as a coordination layer, not as a replacement for durable alliance records. Important scans, standing orders, and information that must survive a busy chat should still be stored somewhere members can reliably find later.
